## 1. Primary critique papers

### 1.1 EvalPlus

Liu, J., Xia, C. S., Wang, Y., & Zhang, L. (2023). *Is Your Code Generated by ChatGPT Really Correct? Rigorous Evaluation of Large Language Models for Code Generation.* In Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 36 (NeurIPS 2023), pp. 21558–21572.

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01210 — **OK**
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-0943 — **OK** (resolves to Proceedings.com)
- Proceedings record: https://www.proceedings.com/075280-0943.html — **OK**
- Artifact: https://github.com/evalplus/evalplus — **OK** (Apache-2.0; v0.3.1, 2024-10-20)
- Dataset/harness documentation: https://github.com/evalplus/evalplus#usage — **OK**

**Verified details:** 80× more tests for HumanEval+; 35× more tests for MBPP+; MBPP+ 378 tasks after v0.2.0; HumanEval+ catches previously undetected wrong code and reduces pass@k by up to 19.3–28.9%.

### 1.2 SWE-bench+

Aleithan, R., Xue, H., Mohajer, M. M., Nnorom, E., Uddin, G., & Wang, S. (2024). *SWE-Bench+: Enhanced Coding Benchmark for LLMs.* arXiv:2410.06992.

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06992 — **OK**
- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13879453 — **OK**
- Zenodo record: https://zenodo.org/records/13879453 — **OK**
- Dataset file: `swe_bench+_data.json` (9,543,869 bytes; 548 instances; CC BY 4.0; creator “Anonymous”)

**Verified details:** 32.67% of successful patches involve solution leakage; 31.08% of passed patches are suspicious due to weak tests; resolution rate falls from 12.47% to 3.97% after filtering; the same issues exist in SWE-bench Lite and SWE-bench Verified. No peer-reviewed venue record was located in the checks performed; describe it as an arXiv preprint.

### 1.3 GSM1k (secondary only)

Zhang, H., Da, J., Lee, D., Robinson, V., Wu, C., Song, W., Zhao, T., Raja, P., Zhuang, C., Slack, D., Lyu, Q., Hendryx, S., Kaplan, R., Lunati, M., & Yue, S. (2024). *A Careful Examination of Large Language Model Performance on Grade School Arithmetic.* arXiv:2405.00332.

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00332 — **OK**
- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScaleAI/gsm1k — **OK**
- Dataset API: https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/ScaleAI/gsm1k — **OK**

**Verified details:** 1,205 rows in the `test` split; fields `question` and `answer`; created 2025-03-31; license field blank on the dataset card; designed to mirror GSM8K’s style and complexity; accuracy drops of up to 8%.

**Use:** secondary task-fit contrast only; excluded from the primary adoption denominator.

## 9. Reference integrity notes

1. **Author lists:** EvalPlus authors were verified from the Proceedings.com record; SWE-bench+ and GSM1k author lists were parsed from the arXiv abs pages on 2026-11-13. HumanEval, MBPP, and SWE-bench lists are standard but were not re-parsed field by field in this pass; check them against the arXiv pages before final submission.
2. **Page numbers:** EvalPlus pages 21558–21572 are verified from Proceedings.com. Other page numbers are omitted rather than guessed.
3. **Dataset authorship:** SWE-bench+ Zenodo creator is “Anonymous”; do not invent named authors for the dataset record. GSM1k is credited to Scale AI on Hugging Face.
4. **Preprint status:** SWE-bench+ has no located peer-reviewed record; cite it as arXiv. Merzah is replication materials, not a peer-reviewed paper.
5. **Link rot:** publisher and API links can change. Re-check at submission and record the date in `verification/journal_requirements.md`.
6. **No fabricated DOIs:** every DOI listed here resolved or returned the expected publisher page on 2026-11-13, except where marked BLOCKED (the DOI itself is standard) or UNRESOLVED (QSS).
