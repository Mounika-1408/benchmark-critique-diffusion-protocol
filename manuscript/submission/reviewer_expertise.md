# Reviewer Expertise Categories

Do not invent reviewer names, affiliations, or conflicts. If the submission system
asks for suggested reviewers, use these categories to identify real people whose
published work matches the manuscript, and verify their current affiliation and
conflict status.

## 1. Benchmark evaluation and reproducibility

**Why:** The paper's construct is documented adoption of benchmark remediations.
Reviewers need expertise in how benchmarks are constructed, versioned, and
reported.

**Relevant expertise:** HumanEval, MBPP, SWE-bench, benchmark contamination,
test-suite adequacy, evaluation harnesses, reproducibility.

## 2. Empirical software engineering and mining software repositories

**Why:** The frame is a citation-based sample of SE/ML papers, and the outcome is
evaluation practice in software engineering research.

**Relevant expertise:** MSR, citation analysis, empirical study design, sampling,
coding protocols, inter-rater reliability.

## 3. Science of science and citation studies

**Why:** The paper distinguishes citation as attention from adoption as practice
change, and it uses a citation frame with title clustering.

**Relevant expertise:** bibliometrics, citation context, diffusion of innovations,
research assessment, semantic scholar / OpenAlex metadata.

## 4. Benchmark dataset curation and licensing

**Why:** SWE-bench+ and EvalPlus differ in artifact form, cost, and license. A
reviewer with dataset-curation experience can evaluate the cost/compatibility
argument and the licensing discussion.

**Relevant expertise:** dataset versioning, benchmark licensing, data statements,
curation and filtering, artifact reuse.

## 5. LLM-assisted research methods

**Why:** The pilot used LLM coding and an LLM second pass. The manuscript is
explicit that this is not human reliability.

**Relevant expertise:** LLM annotation, human-in-the-loop coding, measuring
agreement, prompt provenance, reproducibility of LLM-assisted research.

## Conflicts to screen

- Authors of EvalPlus, SWE-bench+, SWE-bench, GSM1k, or the benchmark papers.
- Authors of the coded pilot papers.
- Anyone with a recent co-authorship or institutional conflict with the author.
- Anyone whose work is the explicit target of the critique.

## Practical note

The current manuscript is not ready for review. Reviewer suggestions should be
prepared only after the full study and human reliability analysis are complete, or
after the paper is reframed as a pilot/methods contribution.
